How your story becomes a book

1. Share

We discuss the people, memories, photographs, letters, and recordings you want to preserve.

2. Shape

I develop a narrative outline and sample visual direction for your approval.

3. Create

I produce sequential illustrated pages with dialogue, captions, and agreed revisions.

4. Preserve

You receive the finished digital story, ready to share or arrange for printing.

Historical projects distinguish documented facts from family recollections and reconstructed dialogue.
