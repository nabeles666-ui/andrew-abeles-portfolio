How this work should be read

Art gets attention. Evidence earns trust.

These works use different storytelling forms. Historical claims should be checked against reliable documentation; dialogue and imagined encounters in fictional comics are creative devices, not verbatim historical records. Where sourcing is incomplete, the work should not be mistaken for a scholarly reference.

Expose the claim

Identify exactly what is being alleged, without repeating a falsehood as fact.

Tell a memorable story

Use character, illustration and satire to make complex material accessible.

Invite verification

Distinguish sourced history from dramatization, and add references as episodes are documented.
